at the threshold // index.htm

Here is a house built of damp paper and patience. It does not hurry, and neither should you. Leave your boots at the door; the floor is all moss now, and it remembers every footfall. What grows here was planted slowly — a page, a pressed leaf, a small quiet spell — and it is left to soften at its own pace, the way all tended things eventually must.

This is a template dressed as a living shrine: swap the copy, hang your own specimens, re-seed the counter, point the links wherever your own grottos lie. Everything below works with the power off — the moon is reckoned by arithmetic, the oracle keeps its own small deck, and the visitor’s book remembers for as long as the window stays open.

a small charm // for a restless house

✿ to quiet a room that will not settle ✿
  • one sprig mugwort, dried until it whispers
  • a pinch of hearth-ash, cold three nights
  • salt that has sat out under one full moon
  • a grey thread, long as your forearm

Bind the sprig in the thread and name, under your breath, the thing the room is holding. Walk the walls once, widdershins, trailing ash at the corners. Hang the bundle where the draught finds it. When it has gone to powder, the room has let go — and so, perhaps, have you. Sweep the remains outward, never in.
