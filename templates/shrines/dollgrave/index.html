enter quietly.

hi. you found my corner. i built it out of table tags and insomnia sometime around 1999 and i never quite left it. everything in here still works even though i don't.

take your shoes off. look at my things. the diary auto-saves nothing, the guestbook forgets you when you close the tab, and the counter has been lying about my visitors since the beginning. nothing here is real except the feeling.

a note on the chrome

built for netscape navigator 4 at 800×600. if the page looks broken, that's correct — it was always a little broken. no cookies, no trackers, no javascript that phones home. just a room.
