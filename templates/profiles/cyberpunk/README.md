# cyberpunkhtml

a free-to-use html theme for neocities, with neon colors, glitch effects, sidebars, and a rotating cube.

[download the theme](https://github.com/numbpill3d/cyberpunkhtml/archive/refs/heads/main.zip) · [browse my neocities themes](https://github.com/numbpill3d#neocities-theme-gallery)

## use on neocities

1. download and unzip the theme files.
2. open `index.html` in your browser to preview the theme.
3. edit the text and links in `index.html`. the styles are inside its `<style>` section.
4. replace any sample image paths with your own images.
5. back up your current neocities files, then upload `index.html` and any images you added through your neocities dashboard.
6. visit your site and check the layout, links, and interactions.

## make it yours

change the colors, text, navigation, and images to fit your own little corner of the web. no build step is needed.
