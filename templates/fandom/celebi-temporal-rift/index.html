THE TEMPORAL TOWER
At the apex of the world stands the Temporal Tower, home to Dialga and the source from which all time flows. When it began to crumble, the planet's paralysis spread outward in waves — like ink dropped into still water. Celebi knew. She had always known.
THE DARK FUTURE
In the timeline we sought to prevent: the sky had turned amber and still. Pokemon moved in loops. Time had crystallized. Primal Dialga, stripped of reason, became time itself — cold and indifferent and absolute. Grovyle was there. He remembered.
CELEBI AND THE RELIC FRAGMENT
The Relic Fragment is a shard of the Hidden Land's ancient inscription plate. It pulses with Celebi's energy — a key shaped like a memory. Those who carry it dream of pink light and the sound of leaves in a wind that comes from no direction.
THE HIDDEN LAND
Accessible only through Brine Cave's deepest chamber, the Hidden Land exists in a fold between present and past. Time moves differently here. Flowers bloom and un-bloom. Celebi watches from the canopy. The staircase to the Temporal Tower begins here — and so does everything.
ON FORGETTING
When the timeline was corrected, the human who had become a Pokemon began to fade. Memory is a function of time — and time, now repaired, had no place for them. Celebi wept. The partner wept. And the world woke up to a morning it had almost never had.
